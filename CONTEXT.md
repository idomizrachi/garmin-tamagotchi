# Watch Companion

The language of a Garmin watch face featuring a creature that shares the wearer's day and takes an encouraging interest in their training.

## Language

**Companion**:
The creature on the watch face, combining an everyday presence with the role of a training buddy. It has its own daily habits while sharing the wearer's active moments and recovery, without claiming to represent how the wearer feels.
_Avoid_: Avatar, virtual pet

**Signature companion**:
The single hooded humanoid creature whose appearance and gestures define the watch face, with a visible warm round face, expressive eyes, a small mouth, subtle cheeks, stubby limbs, and two ear-like points on its soft hood. A selectable cast and cosmetic variants are outside the initial scope.

**Personality**:
The companion's mostly cozy disposition, punctuated by occasional mischievous behavior. Its responses to the wearer's real day are part of its sense of being alive.

**Supportive behavior**:
The companion celebrates movement and milestones while remaining content on quiet days. Missed goals and inactivity never cause disappointment, guilt, or pressure from the companion.

**Daily routine**:
The companion's progression through waking, daytime habits, evening wind-down, and sleep. Habits vary spontaneously within loose, context-appropriate windows rather than following a fixed timetable; recent wearer activity can temporarily reshape the routine outside lunch and the evening schedule.

**Sleep schedule**:
The wearer's chosen usual waking and sleeping times for the companion. Evening sleepiness is distinct from bedtime. Activity does not delay bedtime.

**Usual sleep window**:
The companion becomes sleepy at 20:00 and sleeps from 21:00 until waking at 07:00. It eats lunch from 12:00 until 13:00 local time. The companion remains asleep during the night rather than greeting the wearer; the wearer's sleep focus normally hides the face overnight.

**Modern pixel art**:
The companion's visual style: deliberate pixel shapes with clean silhouettes, expressive poses, and a restrained palette, blending nostalgic pixel character with a contemporary finish.

**Signature palette**:
Muted teal for the companion's hood, warm cream for its visible face, subtle peach cheeks, and small amber accents for props, against a black background.

**Balanced watch face**:
A composition with a small date above the large time, followed by a clearly visible central companion. Three supporting fields follow the lower circular edge in a staggered arrangement: weekly running distance on the left, daily steps on the right, and weather lower in the center.

**Time display**:
The large digital clock using 24-hour time.

**Supporting data fields**:
The compact information accompanying time, date, and the companion: weekly running distance in kilometres, weather, and daily steps. Floors are outside the initial set.

**Weather display**:
Today's high and low temperatures in degrees Celsius, accompanied by weather influence on the surrounding scene only. Weather does not change the companion's behavior.

**Running week**:
The Sunday-through-Saturday calendar period used to group weekly running distance. The distance is informational, with no weekly target or weekly-distance milestone celebrations.

**Daily continuity**:
The companion's memory of the current day's events and milestones, allowing later behavior to acknowledge earlier moments without repeating a celebration on every glance. Each day starts fresh; lasting progression is outside the initial scope.

**Acknowledgment**:
An occasional moment when the companion appears to notice the wearer looking at the watch, such as a glance or tiny wave. Most glances leave its ongoing routine uninterrupted.

**Subtle animation**:
Brief movements such as a blink, sip, or stretch that settle into a readable pose. Larger gestures are reserved for milestones rather than appearing on most glances.

**Resource priority**:
Battery life and responsive watch-face performance take precedence over animation frequency and scene richness. The companion's personality should remain legible through expressive settled poses when motion or detail must be reduced.

**Training activity**:
A workout the wearer records on the Garmin, with running and strength training both relevant to the companion. Everyday movement is distinct from a recorded workout; unavailable or unclear workout information leaves the companion in its ordinary routine rather than prompting a guessed workout response.

**Personal training convention**:
For this wearer's overwhelmingly running-and-strength workout mix, a recorded Training sport is treated as strength for companion behavior. This is a personal interpretation of the broad sport category, not verified strength-subtype detection.

**Workout recovery**:
A brief celebration followed by roughly 30–60 minutes of water breaks, stretching, and rest before the companion resumes its daily routine. The workout response remains visible across multiple glances rather than being consumed by the first glance.

**Delayed acknowledgment**:
A brief celebration shown when the wearer first returns after an unseen workout, even if its recovery window has elapsed. It then yields to the current daily routine rather than starting a late recovery period.

**Always-on view**:
The dimmed watch face showing time and a small resting companion. The full data display and subtle gestures return when the display wakes.

**Primary watch**:
The Forerunner 570 47 mm, which anchors the watch face's design. Initial support also includes a small, explicitly verified set of similar round AMOLED watches.

**Personal-use scope**:
The watch face is intended for the wearer's own use rather than a public Connect IQ release.

**Stage**:
The companion's constant location, defined by a simple ground line, sparse grass tufts, pebbles, and a small distant ridge, plus contextual props. Weather can affect the surrounding scene without changing the location.

**Lingering prop**:
A single temporary object left on the stage after an event, such as a finished coffee mug or a post-workout water bottle. It is later put away, providing continuity without accumulating clutter.

**Everyday habits**:
The companion's coffee breaks, reading, quietly watching the scene, stretching, hood adjustments, and curious looks. Occasional antics include balancing its mug, almost dozing while seated, and proudly flexing a tiny arm.

**Movement response**:
An occasional small change, such as standing, strolling, or stretching, following a sustained burst of everyday walking. Reaching the daily step goal earns a special celebration; quiet periods remain comfortable.

**Automatic behavior**:
The companion's routine, gestures, and responses unfold without taps or other wearer interaction.

## Example dialogue

**Designer:** Is the creature only interested in workouts?
**Wearer:** No. The companion shares my ordinary day and also takes an interest in my training.
