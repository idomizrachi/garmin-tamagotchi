# v0.1 design scope

Personal-use watch face, designed first for the Forerunner 570 47 mm. Battery life and responsive performance take precedence over visual richness. Other similar round AMOLED watches follow verification on the primary device.

## Visual baseline

Use [the accepted staggered concept](staggered-watch-face-concept.png) for composition: large 24-hour time, central companion, running distance at lower left, steps at lower right, and weather lower in the center. The wearer's later refinement places the small date above the time, retaining the clock's existing position and a small gap between them. Preserve the original character's warm visible face and soft teal hood when translating the concept into sprites.

The companion uses modern pixel art, a teal hood with two ear-like points, cream face, peach cheeks, and restrained amber accents. Its fixed stage has a ground line with sparse grass tufts, pebbles, and a small distant ridge, following the wearer's later request for lightweight environment details. Draw these as a few small pixel primitives rather than a full-size background bitmap. At most one temporary prop lingers after an event. Always-on mode shows dim time and a small resting companion without scenery.

## Data

- Weekly running distance in kilometres, Sunday–Saturday, without a weekly goal.
- Daily steps.
- Today's high and low temperatures in Celsius.
- Weather affects scenery only, not companion behavior.

## Small complete daily cycle

- Waking and stretching around the chosen 07:00 wake time.
- Morning coffee within a loose morning window.
- A few idle poses and subtle movements, with occasional acknowledgment of the wearer.
- Small responses to sustained everyday movement and a step-goal celebration.
- Recorded running and strength workout responses: interpret Running as running and Training as strength under the wearer's personal convention, subject to checking actual watch records. Rare non-strength Training sessions receive the same strength response.
- Brief workout celebration visible across multiple glances, then approximately 30–60 minutes of recovery tied to the workout time.
- Brief delayed acknowledgment of an unseen workout, followed by the current routine if recovery has already elapsed.
- Evening sleepiness from 20:00, leading to sleep at 21:00; activity does not delay bedtime.
- Lunch from 12:00 until 13:00 local time.
- Overnight sleep without greetings. The wearer's sleep focus normally hides the face.

Behavior varies within appropriate windows and maintains continuity across glances. It is always supportive, automatic, and remembers the current day without lasting progression. Unclear or missing workout information leaves the ordinary routine in place.

## Later gesture expansion

Reading, hood adjustments, and richer mischievous antics can follow once the core transitions work naturally. No interactive care, selectable character cast, weekly running goals, or public release is planned for v0.1.

## Feasibility checks before implementation commitments

The [API feasibility review](feasibility.md) records documented support, proposed fallbacks, and the simulator/on-watch checks that remain. Training is interpreted as strength for this personal-use version, rather than claiming exact subtype recognition; native weekly running distance is the preferred first route, subject to verifying the Sunday week boundary.

- Availability and completeness of running history for Sunday–Saturday totals.
- Reliable distinction of recorded running and strength sessions on the primary watch.
- Access to today's weather high/low and scene conditions, including stale or missing data behavior.
- Wake, animation, and always-on constraints on the primary watch.
- Actual watch-size legibility, battery use, and responsiveness.

The mockup is visual guidance, not evidence that these checks have passed.
